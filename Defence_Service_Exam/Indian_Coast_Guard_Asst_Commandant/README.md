# Indian Coast Guard Asst Commandant

## Detailed Overview
- **Organiser**: Coast Guard
- **Primary Category**: Defence Service Exam
- **Difficulty Tier**: Advanced
- **Target Career Path**: Defence Officer
- **Optimal Attempt Timeline**: Final year
- **Brief Details**: Written + PPT + SSB + medical; technical/general duty branches

---

## 1. 📚 SYLLABUS
- **English Language**: Reading Comprehension, Synonyms, Antonyms, Spotting Errors, Sentence Improvement, Cloze Test.
- **General Knowledge**: History (Ancient, Medieval, Modern), Geography (Physical, Indian, World), Indian Polity, Economy, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Current Affairs (Defence focus).
- **Elementary Mathematics**: Arithmetic (Percentage, Profit/Loss, Time/Work), Algebra, Geometry, Mensuration, Trigonometry.
- **Military/Technical Aptitude (AFCAT EKT)**: Core engineering subjects (for Technical branch entry).
- **SSB (Stage II)**: Officer Intelligence Rating (OIR), Picture Perception & Description Test (PP&DT), Psychology Tests, GTO Tasks, Interview, Conference.

---

## 2. 📝 EXAM PATTERN
- **Sections**: Written Exam (English, General Knowledge, Mathematics) followed by 5-day SSB interview.
- **Total Marks**: Written: 200–300 marks. SSB: 900 marks.
- **Duration**: 2 hours per written paper. SSB: 5 Days.
- **Negative Marking**: -0.33 or -1 mark per wrong answer.
- **Weightage & Priority**: Written exam is qualifying, but higher marks in written ensure recommendation in the final merit list. SSB selection is the ultimate deciding factor.

---

## 3. 🎓 ELIGIBILITY
- **Academic Criteria**: B.Tech (any branch), 21–25 yrs
- **Age Limit**: Varies (19 to 26 years typically).
- **Attempt Limits**: No limit up to the maximum age threshold.
- **Physical Standards**: Strict height, weight, eyesight (usually 6/6 or corrected 6/9), and medical fitness criteria.

---

## 4. 🎯 RECOMMENDED SCORE / CUTOFF
- **Written Cutoff (General Category)**:
  - CDS: OTA (~100/200), AFA (~140/300), IMA (~120/300).
  - AFCAT: Written (~150/300), EKT (~15-20/50).
  - SSB Recommendation: Absolute score of suitability (recommended/not recommended). Only ~5-8% clear SSB.

---

## 6. 💡 TOP 5 TIPS
1. Read a national newspaper (e.g., The Hindu, Indian Express) daily to build analytical views for GK and SSB GDs.
2. Build physical fitness early: running 1.6 km in 7 mins, push-ups, and chin-ups are standard expectations.
3. Practice speed-math; the elementary mathematics paper in CDS requires completing 100 questions in 120 minutes.
4. Be authentic in the SSB Psychology tests; psychologist assessors catch scripted or coached responses immediately.
5. Develop strong communication skills and basic general awareness to command respect in the Group Discussions.

---

## 7. ⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES
- Ignoring general awareness and current affairs thinking B.Tech engineering core is enough.
- Preparing for SSB interview only after clearing the written exam (officer-like qualities take months to build).
- Getting aggressive or dominating during the Group Obstacle Tasks or Group Discussions in SSB.

---

## ⏳ TIMELINE & DURATION
- **Preparation Timeline**: 4 to 6 months of dedicated preparation.
