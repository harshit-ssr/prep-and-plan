# RRB JE — Railway Junior Engineer

## Detailed Overview
- **Organiser**: RRB / Indian Railways
- **Primary Category**: Technical Govt Exam
- **Difficulty Tier**: Intermediate
- **Target Career Path**: Govt Engineer
- **Optimal Attempt Timeline**: Final year
- **Brief Details**: Junior Engineer in Railways; CBT 1 + CBT 2; strong job security; ₹35–40k starting

---

## 1. 📚 SYLLABUS
- **Engineering Mathematics**: Linear Algebra, Calculus, Differential Equations, Probability & Statistics, Numerical Methods.
- **General Aptitude**: Verbal Ability, Quantitative Ability, Analytical Ability, Spatial Ability.
- **Core Engineering Subjects** (Example for CSE): Data Structures & Algorithms, Theory of Computation, Compiler Design, Operating Systems, Databases (DBMS), Computer Networks, Digital Logic, Computer Organization & Architecture.
- **Govt/Scientific GK (for Scientific entries)**: R&D policies, scientific updates, intellectual property rights.

---

## 2. 📝 EXAM PATTERN
- **Sections**: Technical Core (70%–85% weightage) + Engineering Mathematics + General Aptitude.
- **Total Questions & Marks**: 65 questions, 100 marks (for GATE). ESE: Prelims (500 marks) + Mains (600 marks).
- **Duration**: 3 hours (GATE / ESE Mains).
- **Negative Marking**: -1/3 for 1-mark MCQs, -2/3 for 2-mark MCQs. No negative marking for Numerical Answer Type (NAT) and Multiple Select Questions (MSQ).
- **Priority**: Focus heavily on high-weightage subjects (e.g., Math, Aptitude, DSA, OS, DBMS).

---

## 3. 🎓 ELIGIBILITY
- **Academic Criteria**: B.Tech (relevant branch), 18–33 yrs
- **Age Limit**: GATE has no age limit. ISRO: ≤35 years. DRDO: ≤28 years. SSC JE: ≤32 years.
- **Branch Restrictions**: B.Tech in relevant core branch (CSE, ECE, EEE, Civil, Mechanical, etc.).

---

## 4. 🎯 RECOMMENDED SCORE / CUTOFF
- **GATE Cutoff (CSE/General)**: Qualifying score: ~25-30 marks. Top IIT/PSU Call Score: 750+ (Rank under 200–500).
- **ISRO/DRDO Written Cutoff**: Usually 60%–70% of the total written test marks.
- **SSC JE Prelims Cutoff**: ~110–130 marks out of 200.

---

## 5. 🌐 BEST FREE RESOURCES
- **YouTube Channels**: GATE Academy, NPTEL Lectures, Gate Applied Course, Made Easy, Knowledge Gate, Ravindrababu Ravula.
- **Websites**: GateOverflow (for PYQ discussions), GeeksforGeeks (computer science core), Neso Academy (electronics/electrical basics).
- **GitHub Repos**: Standard GATE CSE study materials repositories.
- **PDFs**: Standard reference textbooks (e.g., Galvin for OS, Korth for DBMS, Cormen for Algorithms).

---

## 6. 💡 TOP 5 TIPS
1. Start with Engineering Mathematics and General Aptitude; they contribute 28-30% of the total score and are high-scoring.
2. Solve Previous Years Questions (PYQs) at least 3 times; understand the concepts behind every wrong choice.
3. Join a high-quality online Test Series (e.g., Made Easy, GATE Academy, Ace) and take tests subject-wise.
4. Analyze mock test performance; maintain a 'Mistake Book' to write down formulas and concepts you get wrong.
5. Maintain speed and accuracy in numerical answer types (NATs); read the rounding-off instructions carefully.

---

## 7. ⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES
- Attempting to cover 100% of the syllabus while leaving no time for revision and mock tests.
- Leaving NAT questions unattempted (they have zero negative marking; guess work within reasonable limits is safe).
- Consulting too many books and study materials instead of sticking to standard reference notes.

---

## ⏳ TIMELINE & DURATION
- **Preparation Timeline**: 8 to 12 months of daily 4-6 hours study.
