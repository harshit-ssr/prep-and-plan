# Master Guide & Index: B.Tech Careers Prep and Plan

Welcome to the **B.Tech Career Guidance Master Registry**. This repository is structured to help Indian B.Tech engineering students plan their career trajectories, whether they aim for the Tech industry, Defense services, Government engineering posts, Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs), Banking sector roles, or top-tier MBA institutes.

---

## 📝 Individual Guide Schema

Every individual exam guide (`README.md`) in this repository contains the following detailed sections:
1. **SYLLABUS**: Full topic-wise breakdown
2. **EXAM PATTERN**: Sections, marks, duration, negative marking, weightage, and priority
3. **ELIGIBILITY**: Academic %, age, attempt limits, and branch restrictions
4. **RECOMMENDED SCORE / CUTOFF**: General category target score
5. **BEST FREE RESOURCES**: YouTube channels, websites, GitHub repos, and PDFs
6. **TOP 5 TIPS**: Most effective strategies specific to the exam
7. **COMMON MISTAKES**: What most aspirants get wrong
8. **TIMELINE**: How many months of preparation needed from scratch

## 🗺️ Master Guidance by Career Goals

### 1. 💻 High-Growth Tech (Software Engineer / FinTech / R&D)
If you want to become a high-paid Software Engineer, you must focus on:
- **Competitive Coding Platforms** (LeetCode, Codeforces) to build logical depth starting in your 1st-2nd year.
- **Corporate Hackathons** (Flipkart GRiD, Goldman Sachs GSIH, Amazon HackOn) in your 3rd year for direct Pre-Placement Interviews (PPIs).
- **Corporate Employability Tests** (TCS NQT, Infosys InfyTQ) in your final year as a fallback mass recruiter option.

### 2. 🏛️ Core Engineering & R&D (Government / PSUs / Defense)
For highly secure, high-prestige roles:
- **GATE** is the holy grail. Prepares you for **PSU Recruitment** (NTPC, ONGC, PGCIL, BHEL) and scientific research positions (**ISRO, BARC, DRDO**). Start prep in the 3rd year.
- **UPSC ESE / IES** for Class-1 officer engineering roles (Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics).
- **Defence Entry Exams** (CDS, AFCAT, TGC, SSC Tech) for commissions as officers in the Army, Navy, or Air Force.

### 3. 📈 Management & Finance (Manager / Banker / Finance-Tech)
For banking, finance, and corporate management:
- **MBA Entrance** (CAT, XAT, SNAP) for admissions to IIMs and other top B-schools. B.Tech graduates are highly valued for their quantitative skills.
- **Regulatory/Apex Banking Exams** (RBI Grade B, SEBI Grade A, NABARD Grade A) for prestigious regulatory roles.
