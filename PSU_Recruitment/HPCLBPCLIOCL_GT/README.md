# HPCL/BPCL/IOCL GT

## Detailed Overview
- **Organiser**: Oil PSUs
- **Primary Category**: PSU Recruitment
- **Difficulty Tier**: Advanced
- **Target Career Path**: Govt Engineer
- **Optimal Attempt Timeline**: Final year
- **Brief Details**: Petroleum/refinery PSU Engineer; GATE shortlisting; ₹12–18 LPA

---

## 1. 📚 SYLLABUS
- **Technical Core**: GATE syllabus of the respective engineering branch.
- **General Aptitude & Reasoning**: Quant (Profit/Loss, Time/Work, Speed/Distance), Reasoning (Blood relations, directions, syllogisms), English Verbal.
- **General Awareness**: Current national events, PSU operations, energy/power/petroleum sector updates, economy basics.
- **HR & Professional Ethics**: Workplace scenarios, corporate communication.

---

## 2. 📝 EXAM PATTERN
- **Selection Method**: Shortlisting via GATE score (typically 80%–85% weightage) OR dedicated Computer Based Test (CBT).
- **CBT Pattern (for non-GATE entries)**: 120–150 MCQs, 50% Technical + 50% Non-technical.
- **Duration**: 2 to 2.5 hours.
- **Negative Marking**: Varies. Many self-conducted PSU tests (like HPCL) have NO negative marking.
- **GD/Interview**: GD/Group Task (5%–10% weightage) + Personal Interview (10%–15% weightage).

---

## 3. 🎓 ELIGIBILITY
- **Academic Criteria**: B.Tech (relevant), GATE score
- **Age Limit**: Generally 25 to 28 years (relaxable for OBC/SC/ST).
- **Attempt Limits**: None up to the age limit.
- **Branch Restrictions**: Strict branch requirements (CE/ME/EE/ECE/CSE).

---

## 4. 🎯 RECOMMENDED SCORE / CUTOFF
- **GATE Rank Cutoff for PSU Call**:
  - Mechanical/Electrical: AIR < 150-300.
  - CSE/ECE: AIR < 100-200.
  - Civil: AIR < 100.
- **Self-exam Cutoff (CBT)**: Typically 70%–75% overall marks required due to high competition.

---

## 5. 🌐 BEST FREE RESOURCES
- **YouTube Channels**: Gate Academy, Unacademy GATE, Made Easy, BYJU'S Exam Prep.
- **Websites**: GeeksforGeeks, Glassdoor (for Interview transcripts), Official PSU Recruitment portals.
- **PDFs**: Made Easy PSU MCQ Banks, standard engineering formula Handbooks.

---

## 6. 💡 TOP 5 TIPS
1. Understand the specific PSU's business profile (e.g., if ONGC, read about drilling/oil production in India).
2. Ensure your B.Tech major project is thoroughly prepared; interviewer will ask detailed conceptual questions on it.
3. Practice speaking in a Group Discussion; focus on logical points, data, and avoid interrupting others.
4. Maintain high accuracy in the CBT aptitude sections since the tech section is often moderate and common to all.
5. Keep basic definitions of core engineering terms on your fingertips for quick interview responses.

---

## 7. ⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES
- Neglecting Group Discussion (GD) preparation, leading to disqualification despite having a top GATE score.
- Not reviewing the basic business functions and current updates (like clean energy targets for NTPC) of the company.
- Assuming the exam difficulty will be higher than GATE (PSU-specific tests are faster, simpler, and formula-based).

---

## ⏳ TIMELINE & DURATION
- **Preparation Timeline**: 6 to 8 months (parallel with GATE preparation).
