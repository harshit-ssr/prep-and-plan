# Flipkart GRiD

## Detailed Overview
- **Organiser**: Flipkart
- **Primary Category**: Corporate Hackathon
- **Difficulty Tier**: Advanced
- **Target Career Path**: Software Engineer
- **Optimal Attempt Timeline**: 3rd year
- **Brief Details**: Software Dev / Infosec / Robotics tracks; PPO/internship for top performers

---

## 1. 📚 SYLLABUS
- **Frontend Development**: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, React.js, Next.js, Vue.js, Tailwind CSS, Material UI.
- **Backend Development**: Node.js, Express.js, Django, FastAPI, Spring Boot, Databases (PostgreSQL, MongoDB, MySQL, Redis).
- **AI/ML/Web3 (Advanced)**: TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-Learn, LangChain, Solidity, Ethers.js.
- **DevOps & Cloud**: Docker, Kubernetes, Git/GitHub, AWS/Azure/GCP hosting, CI/CD pipelines.
- **Pitching & Design**: Figma, Wireframing, Pitch Deck Creation, Demo Video editing.

---

## 2. 📝 EXAM PATTERN
- **Stages**:
  1. Abstract/Idea Submission (Problem Statement Analysis, Tech Stack proposal, Wireframes).
  2. Prototype Development (Online / Virtual Coding Hackathon).
  3. Final Presentation & Demo (3-5 mins presentation to industry experts/judges).
- **Marks/Evaluation**: Rated on Innovation (25%), Tech Complexity (25%), Business Viability (25%), and Demo Quality (25%).
- **Duration**: Idea phase (2-4 weeks), Hacking phase (24 to 72 hours continuously).
- **Weightage**: Having a functional, bug-free core workflow is prioritized over half-baked complex features.

---

## 3. 🎓 ELIGIBILITY
- **Academic Criteria**: B.Tech (all years)
- **Team Size**: Typically 2 to 6 members.
- **Age Limit**: None (must be enrolled in a recognized university).
- **Branch Restrictions**: None. Multidisciplinary teams are encouraged.

---

## 4. 🎯 RECOMMENDED SCORE / CUTOFF
- **Selection target**:
  - Idea Stage: Top 5% to 10% of total submissions.
  - Final Stage: Top 3 teams win main prizes. Top 20-50 teams get interview shortlists (PPOs/internships).

---

## 5. 🌐 BEST FREE RESOURCES
- **YouTube Channels**: Traversy Media, freeCodeCamp, JS Mastery, Fireship.
- **Websites**: Devpost, GitHub Explore, Unstop, MLH (Major League Hacking).
- **GitHub Repos**: Awesome-Hackathon templates, Boilerplate repositories for React-Express-MongoDB stack.
- **PDFs**: Industry problem statements and case study reports.

---

## 6. 💡 TOP 5 TIPS
1. Design a clean, beautiful Landing Page first; judges are wowed by visuals.
2. Build a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) with 1 working core feature rather than 10 broken features.
3. Prepare a highly structured 3-minute pitch video explaining the problem, solution, tech stack, and future scalability.
4. Divide and conquer: assign dedicated roles (1 frontend, 1 backend, 1 AI/integration, 1 presenter/designer).
5. Deploy early! Ensure your application is live on Vercel/Netlify/Heroku before presentation day.

---

## 7. ⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES
- Spending 90% of the hacking time on minor CSS alignment and failing to build the working backend/API.
- Failing to read the judge evaluation rubrics, leading to building something cool but irrelevant to the problem statement.
- Starting to code without finalizing the database schema and API endpoints with team members.

---

- **Preparation Timeline**: 1 to 3 months of project-building experience.
