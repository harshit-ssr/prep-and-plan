# IBPS Clerk

## Detailed Overview
- **Organiser**: IBPS
- **Primary Category**: Banking Exam
- **Difficulty Tier**: Intermediate
- **Target Career Path**: Banker
- **Optimal Attempt Timeline**: Final year
- **Brief Details**: Clerical cadre in PSBs; Prelims + Mains (no interview); ₹4–6 LPA; good fallback

---

## 1. 📚 SYLLABUS
- **Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension (VARC)**: Critical Reasoning, Reading Comprehension (diverse topics), Paragraph Summary, Para-jumbles, Odd-one-out.
- **Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning (DILR)**: Tables, Graphs, Charts, Caselets, Arrangements (Linear/Circular), Venn Diagrams, Games & Tournaments, Matrix & Grid puzzles, Binary Logic.
- **Quantitative Aptitude (QA)**: Arithmetic (Number System, Percentages, Ratio, Work, Speed), Algebra (Linear/Quadratic equations, Functions, Logarithms), Geometry & Mensuration, Modern Mathematics (P&C, Probability, Sequence & Series).

---

## 2. 📝 EXAM PATTERN
- **Sections**: VARC, DILR, and QA (in a fixed order with sectional timers).
- **Total Questions**: 66 questions (for CAT).
- **Duration**: 2 hours (40 minutes per section).
- **Marks**: +3 for correct, -1 for incorrect MCQs. 0 for Non-MCQ type (TITA — Type In The Answer) questions.
- **Priority**: A high balanced sectional percentile is needed, rather than maximizing only one section.

---

## 3. 🎓 ELIGIBILITY
- **Academic Criteria**: Any grad, 20–28 yrs
- **Age Limit**: None.
- **Work Experience**: Not mandatory, but yields extra points during the final selection process at IIMs.
- **Attempt Limits**: No limit.

---

## 4. 🎯 RECOMMENDED SCORE / CUTOFF
- **Target Percentiles (General Category)**:
  - Top 3 IIMs (A/B/C): 99.5+ percentile (B.Tech male general candidates need higher due to diversity policies).
  - New IIMs / IIT B-Schools: 95+ percentile.
  - Tier-2 MBA Colleges: 85–90 percentile.
  - Sectional Cutoff: ~80–85 percentile per section.

## 6. 💡 TOP 5 TIPS
1. Practice reading articles from diverse portals (AEON Essays, The Economist) to improve Reading Comprehension speed.
2. In DILR, selection is key: take 5 minutes to scan all sets, pick the easiest 2, and solve them with 100% accuracy.
3. Build strong math foundations: B.Tech students have an advantage in Quant, but shouldn't neglect formal formulas.
4. Start taking weekly mock tests by July/August, and transition to two mocks per week by October.
5. Maintain a journal of DILR concepts; review the grid-building techniques for arrangements regularly.

---

## 7. ⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES
- Focusing entirely on Quant because it is easier for engineers, leading to failing the VARC/DILR sectional cutoff.
- Taking too many mock tests without performing a detailed 3-hour analysis of each mock.
- Refusing to skip a difficult question during the exam, resulting in wasted time and panic.

- **Preparation Timeline**: 6 to 9 months of structured study.
