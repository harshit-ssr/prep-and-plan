# Wipro NLTH

## Detailed Overview
- **Organiser**: Wipro
- **Primary Category**: Corporate Employability Test
- **Difficulty Tier**: Beginner
- **Target Career Path**: Software Engineer
- **Optimal Attempt Timeline**: Final year
- **Brief Details**: Elite National Talent Hunt; aptitude + coding; ₹3.5–4.5 LPA

---

## 1. 📚 SYLLABUS
- **Quantitative Aptitude**: Number System, Percentages, Profit & Loss, Simple & Compound Interest, Time & Work, Time, Speed & Distance, Probability, Permutations & Combinations, Mensuration.
- **Logical Reasoning**: Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Direction Sense, Seating Arrangement, Syllogism, Data Sufficiency, Series & Analogies.
- **Verbal Ability**: Reading Comprehension, Sentence Correction, Synonyms & Antonyms, Fill in the blanks, Grammar, Cloze Test.
- **Programming & Coding**: Basic programming questions, data types, operators, loops, arrays, strings. Advanced coding: Recursion, Trees, Graphs, Sorting, Searching (Ninja/Digital/Specialist profiles require basic to intermediate DSA).

---

## 2. 📝 EXAM PATTERN
- **Sections**: Cognitive Assessment (Aptitude, Logical, Verbal) + Technical/Coding Assessment.
- **Total Marks**: Varies by year (approx. 100–120 questions).
- **Duration**: 90 to 180 minutes.
- **Negative Marking**: No negative marking in most cognitive rounds (adaptive tests like TCS NQT do not allow skipping questions).
- **Weightage & Priority**: Coding carries the highest priority for premium packages (e.g., TCS Prime/Digital, Infosys Specialist, Cognizant GenC Next). Aptitude and English act as primary filters.

---

## 3. 🎓 ELIGIBILITY
- **Academic Criteria**: B.Tech, 60%, no backlogs
- **Age Limit**: Generally 18 to 28 years.
- **Attempt Limits**: No hard limit, but usually restricted to once per hiring season.
- **Branch Restrictions**: B.E. / B.Tech (CS, IT, ECE, EEE, Mechanical, Civil etc.) and sometimes MCA/M.Sc.

---

## 4. 🎯 RECOMMENDED SCORE / CUTOFF
- **Aptitude/Verbal**: 65%–70% sectional score.
- **Coding**:
  - Ninja/GenC (Basic Package): Solved 0–1 coding question out of 2.
  - Digital/GenC Next (Medium Package): Solved 1–2 coding questions out of 2.
  - Prime/Specialist (Premium Package): Solved 2 coding questions with optimal time complexity.

---

## 5. 🌐 BEST FREE RESOURCES
- **YouTube Channels**: PrepInsta, Careerera, OnlineStudy4U, Knowledge Gate, Neso Academy.
- **Websites**: IndiaBIX (Aptitude), GeeksforGeeks (Coding & Aptitude), Sanfoundry (technical MCQs).
- **GitHub Repos**: Repository links containing previous years' coding questions and solution templates.
- **PDFs**: RS Aggarwal (Quantitative Aptitude & Verbal Reasoning).

---

## 6. 💡 TOP 5 TIPS
1. Master basic coding in C++, Java, or Python; know how to handle standard I/O (stdin/stdout).
2. Practice time management on IndiaBIX — cognitive sections are speed-based.
3. Ensure your CGPA stays above 6.0/60% to avoid resume filtering.
4. Solve previous 3 years' memory-based questions, as corporate tests often reuse patterns.
5. Learn basic SQL queries, as Database queries are frequently asked in the tech MCQ section.

---

## 7. ⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES
- Over-relying on coding and completely neglecting basic English grammar and aptitude tests.
- Spending too much time on a single mathematical puzzle during the live, non-skippable exam.
- Not practicing compiling code in the specific test environment platform (e.g., Mercer Mettl, HackerEarth).

---

## ⏳ TIMELINE & DURATION
- **Preparation Timeline**: 2 to 3 months of consistent practice.
